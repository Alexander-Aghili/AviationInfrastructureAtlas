    DATA & PROVENANCE

    A source behind every facility.

    FAA NASR is the primary inventory. Supplemental government publications supply radar positions and facility addresses. FAA-sponsored MIT Lincoln Laboratory reports document equipment models; they do not certify present equipment or operational status.

    NASA/JPL's radar inventory is undated. Its site-description coordinates are used, never the decorative exclusion polygons. NCT and six other TRACON locations are approximate Census street-range geocodes of FAA addresses. Radomes.org supplies clearly labeled historical corroboration for Mt. Tam.

    Every facility separates location evidence, model evidence, dates and source publisher. Unknown positions remain searchable without invented markers. Boundaries show only validated straight segments; no surveillance coverage is inferred. Published coverage overlays and cycle comparisons remain pending validated source files and retained snapshots.
