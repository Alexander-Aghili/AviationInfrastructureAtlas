Subject: ATLANTA SATELLITE RNAV PREFERRED DEPARTURE ROUTINGS

Cancellation: 04/13/2018 1200 (UTC)

The Federal Aviation Administration implemented another phase of the Atlanta Metroplex project on April 12, 2016. Concurrent with the ATL changes were changes to the enroute navigation structure which included Q Routes to the east of the Atlanta metropolitan area. The enroute automation system has been updated with preferential RNAV departure routings from Satellite airports within 40 miles of ATL.
