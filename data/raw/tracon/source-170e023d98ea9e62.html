Subject: VFR Practice Instrument Approaches

Cancellation: 02/01/2023 0859 (UTC)

Minneapolis Approach will provide IFR longitudinal separation and/or a minimum of 500 feet vertical separation to VFR aircraft practicing instrument approaches to airports within Minneapolis Approach Control airspace. Such separation will begin at the point where the approach clearance becomes effective. When not possible to do so, or where conditions dictate terminating separation services already being provided, the aircraft will be instructed to maintain VFR and advised that no separation services will be provided.

Aircraft requesting VFR practice approaches to the airports listed below should contact Minneapolis Approach Control on the appropriate frequency prior to commencing an approach.

Frequency 134.7
Minneapolis/Flying Cloud (FCM)
Minneapolis/Airlake (LVN)
Faribault Municipal (FBL)
Glencoe Municipal (GYL)
