# Aviation Infrastructure Atlas

An interactive map of public US aviation infrastructure, with facility details and links to the records behind them.

- Explore radars, control towers, approach facilities, navigation aids, communications, weather stations, and landing systems.
- Search by name, identifier, equipment type, or location, and switch between map and satellite views.
- Browse air traffic control center boundaries and their associated facilities.
- View modeled radar coverage at different altitudes and share a map view by URL.

## Data

Built from FAA NASR cycle **2026-10-01**, with supplemental FAA, NASA/JPL, MIT Lincoln Laboratory, and NTSB sources. See the [data audit](public/data/audit.json) for inventory counts and limitations.

Approximate locations are labeled. Source dates describe published evidence, not live equipment status. Radar coverage is a terrain line-of-sight estimate; center boundaries are published outlines, not internal controller sectors.

This independent atlas is not an FAA product or a navigation tool.
